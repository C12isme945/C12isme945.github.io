Overview

Laboratory potentiostats and impedance analyzers cost between $5,000 and $15,000, require a benchtop setup and a trained operator. That puts routine water-quality screening out of reach exactly where it matters most: decentralized field testing and point-of-care diagnostics.

BioSmartSense-IoT answers with open hardware: a compact 4-layer HDI board built around the AD5940 precision analog front end, standard screen-printed electrodes, and an edge pipeline that turns raw impedance data into a safety verdict in seconds.

Highlights

01

Lab precision, field cost

Full BOM under $35 using the AD5940’s 16-bit ADC, 800 kSPS sampling and on-chip DFT engine.

02

Wide electrochemical range

EIS from 0.016 Hz to 200 kHz with current ranges from ±28 µA to ±3 mA via programmable RTIAs.

03

Physics-informed ML

Randles equivalent-circuit fitting (Rs, Rct, Cdl, Warburg) feeds a classifier for contaminant levels and water-quality index.

04

Actionable telemetry

Live Nyquist, Bode and cyclic-voltammetry plots with WHO / EPA safe, warning and hazard alerts.

System architecture

SPE electrodes→ AD5940 AFE→ Edge host · SPI→ Randles fitting→ ML classifier→ Safety alert
